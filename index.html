How to send your proof

  1. Choose your locale.
  2. Tap "Open on YouTube". Press Like 👍 and leave a comment. While it's live, comment in the live chat. After the broadcast, comment under the video.
  3. Take a screenshot that shows your comment with your YouTube name. If you can, show the Like button turned on too.
  4. Come back here and send the screenshot.
  5. One proof per phone each week. Each family member with their own phone can send their own.
